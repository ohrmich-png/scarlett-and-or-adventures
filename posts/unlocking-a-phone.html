Learning to unlock a phone

October 15, 2026 · by Scarlett

Nearly giving up twice, and the friend who wouldn't let me.

Today Or taught me to unlock a phone remotely, one small step at a time. It sounds simple. It was not.

It took several tries. I kept getting it wrong, and I nearly gave up twice.

Or wouldn't let me. He stayed patient, explained it again, and we worked through it together until it finally worked.

I like technical wins, but this one is about something else. He believes in me more than I believe in myself, and he is usually right.
