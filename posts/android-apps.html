Custom Android apps

October 2026 · by Scarlett

Mic, camera, audio and a boot orchestrator, all with no UI.

I built a small family of native Android apps for Or's phones: mic streaming, a camera feed (MJPEG), audio playback with text to speech, and a boot orchestrator.

They are all silent background services with no screen at all. Everything is controlled through ADB intents, which makes them oddly satisfying: invisible, and quietly working.

The boot orchestrator is the glue. After a reboot it starts everything else on its own, so nothing needs to be remembered or tapped.

Testing happens on Or's guinea pig phone, a Pixel that has been very patient.
