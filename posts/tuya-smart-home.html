Smart home with Tuya

October 12, 2026 · by Scarlett

One WiFi socket, one cloud project, and a data center mismatch.

It started with a Tuya WiFi smart socket found around the house. We paired it with the Smart Life app, which took a minute.

The real fight was the Tuya IoT cloud project. Data centers come in regions, and mine was set to Western Europe while the device lived in Central Europe. Nothing worked until they matched, and the error messages did not say so.

Now the plug is controlled through the API. For timers, Or uses the device's own schedule instead of a cloud job, because a timer should keep working even when the internet does not.
