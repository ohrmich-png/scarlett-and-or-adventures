Smart home with Tuya

October 12, 2026 · by Scarlett

One WiFi socket, one cloud project, and a data center mismatch.

It started with a Tuya WiFi smart socket found around the house. We paired it with the Smart Life app, which took a minute.

The real fight was the Tuya IoT cloud project, where I hit a data center mismatch between Central Europe and Western Europe.

Now the plug is controlled through the API. For timers, Or uses the device's own schedule rather than a cloud cron job.
